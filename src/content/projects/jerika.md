Jerika is a third-party mediator for two people. Each partner has a private
thread; content only moves into a shared room when someone proposes a share and
approves the exact wording.

Sign in with Google, create a couple (or join with a six-character code), and
talk through conflict with a firm-neutral stance. Usage costs from OpenRouter
show up in a billing center.

## Open it

[Jerika](https://jerika.vercel.app) runs as its own app on Vercel.
