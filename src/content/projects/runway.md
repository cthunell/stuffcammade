Runway is a day-by-day cash forecast for the next twelve months. It projects my
balance from recurring bills, subscriptions, paychecks and a monthly spending
budget, then shows when the balance would first dip below zero.

Everything is adjustable: toggle a bill off to see what cutting it buys, change
the budget by category, or compare different income scenarios. Edits save
automatically, so it always opens where I left off.

The tool itself is private and needs my Google sign-in, since it runs on my real
numbers.
