Runway is a simple monthly budget with a cash forecast built in. You add your
balance, income, bills and an everyday budget, and the dashboard tells you how
much to aim to spend each day so you stay above your safety cushion, plus what
is coming up and where your balance is headed.

Check in weekly or monthly by typing in your balance. Runway compares that to
the forecast so you can see whether everyday spending is tracking over or under
budget.

For tighter stretches, Runway mode shows how long your money would last with no
income and which bills or budget cuts would stretch it the most.

My copy is private and needs my Google sign-in, since it runs on my real numbers.

## Use it yourself

[Try Runway](/runway-template). It starts blank, or you can load sample numbers to
look around. Everything stays in your own browser and is never sent anywhere, and
Settings lets you download a backup of your numbers.
