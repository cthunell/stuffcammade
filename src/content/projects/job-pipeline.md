Job Pipeline tracks each application as a card that moves through numbered stages:
Applied, Scheduling, Interviewing, Waiting to hear back, Offer and Declined. Each card shows
the company, level, what they sell and a short pay line. Opening a card shows the full
details, a next step, notes and the history of every move.

Some moves ask a question first. Moving to Scheduling asks when the interview is (or
whether it is still being scheduled) and how high a priority it is. Leaving Interviewing
asks for a 1 to 10 rating, and moving to Declined asks why the opportunity was lost.

A table view shows the same jobs as rows, with sortable, filterable and reorderable columns.
